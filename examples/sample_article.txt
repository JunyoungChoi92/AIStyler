[TITLE]South Korea to invest W1.5 trillion in A.I. development[/TITLE]
[BODY]The government announced plans to invest 1.5 trillion won ($1.1 billion) in artificial intelligence development over the next five years. The Ministry of Science and ICT said that the investment will focus on developing core AI technologies and nurturing AI talents. "This is a crucial step for South Korea's future competitiveness," said Minister Kim. The ministry also announced that they will establish an AI research center in Seoul next year. According to the plan, 60% of the budget will be allocated to basic research while 40% will support commercialization. South Korea currently ranks 5th globally in AI research output.[/BODY]
[CAPTION]Minister Kim speaks at a press conference at the government complex in Seoul on monday.[/CAPTION]
